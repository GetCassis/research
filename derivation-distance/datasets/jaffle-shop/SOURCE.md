# Source and provenance: Jaffle Shop

## Original data

dbt Labs' **Jaffle Shop**, the canonical fictional dataset, generated by `jafgen`.

- Project: https://github.com/dbt-labs/jaffle-shop (classic: https://github.com/dbt-labs/jaffle_shop_duckdb)
- Generator: https://github.com/dbt-labs/jafgen

## How the bundled data differs from raw `jafgen`

The raw seeds were reshaped into a credible cafe chain (raw `jafgen` gives ~935
customers with a median of 55 orders/year and orders at only 2 of the 6 stores):

- ~6,910 customers rebuilt to a realistic long tail, each homed to one store
- orders spread across all 6 stores (Philadelphia the clear leader)
- tax recomputed per store; money in dollars; dates shifted so the data ends 2026-06-17

The reshape is deterministic (`SEED=20260612`). The `data/` CSVs are the reshaped
tables, and they are what `ontology.json` describes.

## Tables (6, reshaped)

`customers` (6,910), `orders` (61,948), `order_items` (90,900), `products` (10),
`stores` (6), `supplies` (65). UPPERCASE column names, matching the ontology.
