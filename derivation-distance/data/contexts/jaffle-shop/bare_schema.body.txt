# Schema

## ORDERS
- ORDER_ID (VARCHAR)
- CUSTOMER_ID (VARCHAR)
- STORE_ID (VARCHAR)
- ORDERED_AT (TIMESTAMP)
- SUBTOTAL (DOUBLE)
- TAX_PAID (DOUBLE)
- ORDER_TOTAL (DOUBLE)

## ORDER_ITEMS
- ORDER_ITEM_ID (VARCHAR)
- ORDER_ID (VARCHAR)
- SKU (VARCHAR)

## PRODUCTS
- SKU (VARCHAR)
- PRODUCT_NAME (VARCHAR)
- PRODUCT_TYPE (VARCHAR)
- PRICE (DOUBLE)
- DESCRIPTION (VARCHAR)

## SUPPLIES
- SUPPLY_ID (VARCHAR)
- SUPPLY_NAME (VARCHAR)
- COST (DOUBLE)
- PERISHABLE (BOOLEAN)
- SKU (VARCHAR)

## CUSTOMERS
- CUSTOMER_ID (VARCHAR)
- CUSTOMER_NAME (VARCHAR)

## STORES
- STORE_ID (VARCHAR)
- STORE_NAME (VARCHAR)
- OPENED_AT (TIMESTAMP)
- TAX_RATE (DOUBLE)

## Joins
- JAFFLE.ORDERS.CUSTOMER_ID = JAFFLE.CUSTOMERS.CUSTOMER_ID
- JAFFLE.ORDERS.STORE_ID = JAFFLE.STORES.STORE_ID
- JAFFLE.ORDER_ITEMS.ORDER_ID = JAFFLE.ORDERS.ORDER_ID
- JAFFLE.ORDER_ITEMS.SKU = JAFFLE.PRODUCTS.SKU
- JAFFLE.SUPPLIES.SKU = JAFFLE.PRODUCTS.SKU
