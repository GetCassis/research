# Operating context

- The data is a static snapshot; treat the current date ('today') as 2026-06-17 for any relative time window.
- Relative time windows ('last N days') are lower-bounded only: include everything from today minus N days onward, and do not add an upper bound at today.

# Schema

## ORDERS — One row per order: a register transaction at one store by one customer.
- ORDER_ID (VARCHAR): Unique order identifier.
- CUSTOMER_ID (VARCHAR): Customer who placed the order.
- STORE_ID (VARCHAR): Store the order was placed at.
- ORDERED_AT (TIMESTAMP): When the order was placed.
- SUBTOTAL (DOUBLE): Order value before sales tax, in USD. The basis for revenue and order-value figures.
- TAX_PAID (DOUBLE): Sales tax on the order, in USD. The rate varies by store.
- ORDER_TOTAL (DOUBLE): Amount the customer actually paid: subtotal plus sales tax, in USD.

## ORDER_ITEMS — One row per item inside an order.
- ORDER_ITEM_ID (VARCHAR): Unique order item identifier.
- ORDER_ID (VARCHAR): Order this item belongs to.
- SKU (VARCHAR): Menu product this item is. Join to PRODUCTS for name, type, and price.

## PRODUCTS — One row per menu product (jaffles and beverages).
- SKU (VARCHAR): Unique product code. JAF-prefixed are jaffles, BEV-prefixed are beverages.
- PRODUCT_NAME (VARCHAR): Menu name of the product.
- PRODUCT_TYPE (VARCHAR): 'jaffle' (food) or 'beverage' (drink).
- PRICE (DOUBLE): Menu price in USD, before tax.
- DESCRIPTION (VARCHAR): Menu description of the product.

## SUPPLIES — One row per supply used to make a product. Several supplies per product.
- SUPPLY_ID (VARCHAR): Supply identifier. The same supply can appear under several products.
- SUPPLY_NAME (VARCHAR): Name of the supply (ingredient or packaging).
- COST (DOUBLE): Cost of this supply per unit of product made, in USD. A product's unit cost is the SUM of its supply costs.
- PERISHABLE (BOOLEAN): True if the supply is perishable.
- SKU (VARCHAR): Product this supply is used for.

## CUSTOMERS — One row per known customer, identified across visits and stores.
- CUSTOMER_ID (VARCHAR): Unique customer identifier.
- CUSTOMER_NAME (VARCHAR): Customer's full name.

## STORES — One row per Jaffle Shop location.
- STORE_ID (VARCHAR): Unique store identifier.
- STORE_NAME (VARCHAR): City the store is in; used as the store's name.
- OPENED_AT (TIMESTAMP): Date the store opened.
- TAX_RATE (DOUBLE): Sales tax rate applied at this store (for example 0.06 = 6%).

## Joins
- JAFFLE.ORDERS.CUSTOMER_ID = JAFFLE.CUSTOMERS.CUSTOMER_ID  (Each order was placed by one customer.)
- JAFFLE.ORDERS.STORE_ID = JAFFLE.STORES.STORE_ID  (Each order was placed at one store.)
- JAFFLE.ORDER_ITEMS.ORDER_ID = JAFFLE.ORDERS.ORDER_ID  (Each order item belongs to one order.)
- JAFFLE.ORDER_ITEMS.SKU = JAFFLE.PRODUCTS.SKU  (Each order item is one menu product.)
- JAFFLE.SUPPLIES.SKU = JAFFLE.PRODUCTS.SKU  (Each supply row is used for one product; a product has several supply rows.)

# Domain context

## Jaffle Shop

Jaffle Shop is a cafe chain with six locations across the US. It sells jaffles (pressed toasted sandwiches) and beverages. Every sale rings up as an order at one store, placed by one customer, containing one or more items off the menu.

## How the data is organized

- [sales](sales): orders and the items inside them. The menu (products and supply costs) lives in its child domain.
- [customers](customers): who buys, and how to measure customer behavior.
- [stores](stores): the six locations.

The bridge between areas is the order: it links a customer, a store, and items. Any "by store" or "by customer" cut of a sales figure goes through `ORDERS`.

## Rules that apply to every question

- Monetary amounts are in USD.
- Revenue and order values are pre-tax: use `SUBTOTAL`. `ORDER_TOTAL` includes sales tax, which varies by store, so totals including tax are not comparable across stores.
- Core grain hierarchy: a customer places orders at a store; an order contains order items; each item is one menu product.

## Answering business terms

When a question uses a business qualifier or membership term that is not a column (for example "active", "regular", "top", "lapsed", "new"), first open the relevant domain document(s) and use any definition found there. If no document defines the term, do not pick a threshold silently: state the assumption you would make, name the other reasonable readings (threshold and time window), and ask the user to confirm or define it. Prefer asking over guessing whenever the choice would materially change the answer.

## Sales

A sale is one order: a register transaction at one store by one customer, holding one or more items. Item-level questions (what was ordered, product mix) go through `ORDER_ITEMS` joined to the menu; order-level questions (revenue, order counts, basket size) stay on `ORDERS`, whose money columns are already order-level totals. Do not sum item prices to rebuild an order's value; `SUBTOTAL` already carries it.

Child domain:

- [menu](sales/menu): the products on the menu and what they cost us to make.

## Menu

The menu is small and fixed: jaffles and beverages (`PRODUCT_TYPE`). "Best sellers" or product-mix questions count `ORDER_ITEMS` rows by product. The cost side lives in `SUPPLIES`: several supply rows per product, so a product's unit cost is the SUM of its supply costs, never a single row's value.

## Customers

One row per known customer, identified across visits and stores. The table carries identity only; everything behavioral (how often they come, what they spend, when they were last seen) is derived from their orders.

Child domain:

- [measuring customers](customers/measuring): how to compute customer behavior figures from orders.

## Measuring customers

How to turn orders into per-customer figures. None of these are stored; derive them by grouping `ORDERS` by `CUSTOMER_ID`.

- Visit frequency: count a customer's orders over a time window.
- Customer spend: sum a customer's pre-tax `SUBTOTAL` over a window.
- Recency: a customer's most recent `ORDERED_AT`.
- First visit: a customer's earliest `ORDERED_AT`.

A customer's home store is not stored either; if needed, take the store where they have placed the most orders. For store-level customer questions, group the same derivations by `STORE_ID` as well.

## Stores

Six locations, named by city. Store-level cuts of any sales or customer figure go through `ORDERS.STORE_ID`. Stores opened on different dates (`OPENED_AT`), so year-over-year comparisons across stores are only fair for stores open the whole period. Sales tax rate is per store, which is why the same subtotal yields different `ORDER_TOTAL` at different stores.

# Metrics

## Revenue (`revenue`)
- Description: Total order value before sales tax, in USD. The company's headline revenue figure.
- Unit: USD
- Also called: sales, turnover, net sales

## Orders (`order_count`)
- Description: Number of orders.
- Unit: count
- Also called: number of orders, order volume, tickets

## Average order value (`average_order_value`)
- Description: Average pre-tax value of an order, in USD.
- Unit: USD
- Also called: AOV, average basket, average ticket

# Metric implementations

## `revenue`
- Expression: SUM("SUBTOTAL")
- Table: JAFFLE.ORDERS
- Filters: none

## `order_count`
- Expression: COUNT(*)
- Table: JAFFLE.ORDERS
- Filters: none

## `average_order_value`
- Expression: AVG("SUBTOTAL")
- Table: JAFFLE.ORDERS
- Filters: none

# Worked examples (library)

## How many customers placed more orders in the last 90 days than in the 90 days before that?

```sql
WITH per_customer AS (
  SELECT CUSTOMER_ID,
         COUNT(*) FILTER (
           WHERE ORDERED_AT >= DATE '2026-06-17' - INTERVAL 90 DAY) AS last90,
         COUNT(*) FILTER (
           WHERE ORDERED_AT >= DATE '2026-06-17' - INTERVAL 180 DAY
             AND ORDERED_AT <  DATE '2026-06-17' - INTERVAL 90 DAY) AS prior90
  FROM ORDERS
  GROUP BY CUSTOMER_ID
)
SELECT COUNT(*) AS accelerating_customers
FROM per_customer
WHERE last90 > prior90
```

## What is our total revenue?

```sql
SELECT SUM(SUBTOTAL) AS revenue
FROM ORDERS
```

## What is total revenue by month?

```sql
SELECT date_trunc('month', ORDERED_AT) AS month, SUM(SUBTOTAL) AS revenue
FROM ORDERS
GROUP BY month
ORDER BY month
```

## On average, how many days pass between a customer's first order and their most recent order (averaged across customers)?

```sql
WITH per_customer AS (
  SELECT CUSTOMER_ID,
         date_diff('day', MIN(ORDERED_AT), MAX(ORDERED_AT)) AS span_days,
         COUNT(*) AS n_orders
  FROM ORDERS
  GROUP BY CUSTOMER_ID
)
SELECT AVG(span_days) AS avg_span_days
FROM per_customer
WHERE n_orders >= 2
```

## What is the jaffle attach rate, i.e. the share (%) of orders that contain at least one jaffle item?

```sql
SELECT 100.0 * COUNT(*) FILTER (
         WHERE EXISTS (
           SELECT 1 FROM ORDER_ITEMS oi
           JOIN PRODUCTS p ON oi.SKU = p.SKU
           WHERE oi.ORDER_ID = o.ORDER_ID AND p.PRODUCT_TYPE = 'jaffle'
         )) / COUNT(*) AS jaffle_attach_rate_pct
FROM ORDERS o
```

## For each acquisition-month cohort, how many customers are in the cohort and how many orders have they placed in total?

```sql
WITH firsts AS (
  SELECT CUSTOMER_ID,
         date_trunc('month', MIN(ORDERED_AT)) AS cohort_month
  FROM ORDERS
  GROUP BY CUSTOMER_ID
)
SELECT f.cohort_month,
       COUNT(DISTINCT f.CUSTOMER_ID) AS cohort_customers,
       COUNT(o.ORDER_ID) AS cohort_total_orders
FROM firsts f
JOIN ORDERS o ON o.CUSTOMER_ID = f.CUSTOMER_ID
GROUP BY f.cohort_month
ORDER BY f.cohort_month
```

## What is the running cumulative order count by month?

```sql
WITH monthly AS (
  SELECT date_trunc('month', ORDERED_AT) AS month, COUNT(*) AS orders
  FROM ORDERS
  GROUP BY 1
)
SELECT month,
       orders,
       SUM(orders) OVER (ORDER BY month
                         ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW) AS cumulative_orders
FROM monthly
ORDER BY month
```

## What is the month-over-month order-count growth rate (%) for each month?

```sql
WITH monthly AS (
  SELECT date_trunc('month', ORDERED_AT) AS month, COUNT(*) AS orders
  FROM ORDERS
  GROUP BY 1
)
SELECT month,
       orders,
       100.0 * (orders - LAG(orders) OVER (ORDER BY month))
             / LAG(orders) OVER (ORDER BY month) AS growth_pct
FROM monthly
ORDER BY month
```


## What is each product type's share (%) of total product revenue?

```sql
SELECT p.PRODUCT_TYPE,
       SUM(p.PRICE) AS product_revenue,
       100.0 * SUM(p.PRICE) / SUM(SUM(p.PRICE)) OVER () AS pct_of_total
FROM ORDER_ITEMS oi
JOIN PRODUCTS p ON oi.SKU = p.SKU
GROUP BY p.PRODUCT_TYPE
ORDER BY product_revenue DESC
```

## For each store, how many orders did it take in the last 90 days versus the 90 days before that, and the growth rate (%) between them?

```sql
WITH per_store AS (
  SELECT STORE_ID,
         COUNT(*) FILTER (
           WHERE ORDERED_AT >= DATE '2026-06-17' - INTERVAL 90 DAY) AS last90,
         COUNT(*) FILTER (
           WHERE ORDERED_AT >= DATE '2026-06-17' - INTERVAL 180 DAY
             AND ORDERED_AT <  DATE '2026-06-17' - INTERVAL 90 DAY) AS prior90
  FROM ORDERS
  GROUP BY STORE_ID
)
SELECT s.STORE_NAME,
       ps.prior90,
       ps.last90,
       100.0 * (ps.last90 - ps.prior90) / ps.prior90 AS growth_pct
FROM per_store ps
JOIN STORES s ON ps.STORE_ID = s.STORE_ID
ORDER BY growth_pct DESC, s.STORE_NAME
```

## What is the best-selling product (by number of items sold) within each product type (jaffle vs beverage)?

```sql
WITH counts AS (
  SELECT p.PRODUCT_TYPE, oi.SKU, COUNT(*) AS items_sold
  FROM ORDER_ITEMS oi
  JOIN PRODUCTS p ON oi.SKU = p.SKU
  GROUP BY p.PRODUCT_TYPE, oi.SKU
),
ranked AS (
  SELECT counts.*,
         ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY PRODUCT_TYPE ORDER BY items_sold DESC, SKU) AS rn
  FROM counts
)
SELECT ranked.PRODUCT_TYPE, p.PRODUCT_NAME, ranked.items_sold
FROM ranked
JOIN PRODUCTS p ON ranked.SKU = p.SKU
WHERE ranked.rn = 1
ORDER BY ranked.PRODUCT_TYPE
```
